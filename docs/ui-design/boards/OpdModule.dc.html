What makes ours different

One visit, one bill, one token system. Consultation, tests and medicines from the same visit land on one bill, and every counter shares one TV queue.
India-ready. ABHA Scan and Share check-in, UPI payment devices, WhatsApp messages, DLT SMS templates, GST-aware billing and regional-language prescriptions.
Governed by rules, not habits. Free follow-up, no-show, cancellation and walk-in rules are settings. Queue jumps and discounts need a reason and are audited.
Measured end to end. Every step is time-stamped, so wait time per stage and total time in OPD are reported for NABH without manual registers.
Role panels. Each role sees only its own OPD work: the nurse sees triage, the cashier sees billing, the doctor sees the consultation queue.
Ready for Phase 2 AI. Structured data and the message engine are the base for AI follow-up calls, dictation-to-prescription and smart scheduling.

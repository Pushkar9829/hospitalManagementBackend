STEP 1Needs a doctor
Calls, books on the portal or WhatsApp, or walks in
STEP 2Books a slot
Doctor, date and slot; OTP check; pay online if wanted
STEP 3Confirms
SMS and WhatsApp now; reminders 24 h and 2 h before
STEP 4Check-in
Finds UHID or registers; ABHA scan; consents
STEP 5Consultation fee
Collected, or waived by the free follow-up rule
STEP 6Token issued
Joins doctor queue; TV and SMS show position
STEP 7Triage
Vitals, complaint, pain score, priority
STEP 8Consultation
History, examination, diagnosis (ICD-10)
STEP 9Plan
e-Prescription, test orders, advice, admit or refer
STEP 10Bills orders
One bill for all tests; member and package prices
STEP 11Tests done
Sample or scan; report sent to doctor and patient
STEP 12Dispenses
Rx from queue; earliest-expiry batches; counselling
STEP 13Reviews results
Same-day review when needed; updates plan
STEP 14Closes visit
Follow-up booked; feedback link; wait times logged

Time stamps captured on every visit
Booked · arrived · checked in · fee paid · triage start and end · consult start and end · orders billed · sample collected · report released · medicines dispensed · visit closed. These give wait time per stage, doctor consult time and total time in hospital (NABH indicator and Phase 2 analytics).
Parallel work
Steps 10 to 12 can happen in any order: the patient may go to the pharmacy before the lab. Each counter has its own token series (B-, S-, P-) on the same TV screen.
Blue cards
Steps the system does on its own: messages, tokens, queue updates and closing the visit. Nobody has to click.

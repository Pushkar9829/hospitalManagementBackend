Each board on this canvas is one screen. Build them in React 18 + TypeScript + MUI 6 with the tokens below. Routes, permissions and APIs follow the Phase 1 specification PDF. Press Play on any board to click through it.

8 px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 48

Touch targets 44 px (inputs, buttons); table row 40 px; side menu 232 px

Layout and breakpoints

Desktop 1280 to 1440: menu + content. Tablet 768 to 1279: menu collapses to icons or drawer. Phone under 768: menu becomes a drawer, grids stack to one column, wide tables scroll inside their card.

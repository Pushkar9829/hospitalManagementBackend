STEP 1Tests ordered
Order set or single tests; priority routine, urgent or STAT
STEP 2Order billed
OPD pays before collection; IPD to running bill
STEP 3Labels and list
Barcode per container; collection list by ward and time
STEP 4Sample collected
Wristband and label scanned; collector and time saved
STEP 5Received
Label, container, volume checked; accept or reject with reason
STEP 6Routed
To bench and analyser worklists; orders sent to the analyser
STEP 7Run
Only after QC for the run is in control; results uploaded
STEP 8Checks
Range flags, delta check, critical limits
STEP 9Critical value phoned
To doctor or nurse in 30 min; read-back logged
STEP 10Validated
Pathologist reviews and releases (maker-checker)
STEP 11Report delivered
Doctor inbox, patient WhatsApp, ABHA; print with QR
STEP 12Reviews and acts
Result acknowledged; plan changed if needed
STEP 13Sees report
Phone link with trend of past results
STEP 14Sample stored
Kept for the retention period, then discarded as waste
STEP 15Quality logged
TAT by stage, rejections, QC, NABL indicators

Quality gates

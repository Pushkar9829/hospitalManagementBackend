What makes ours different

Money never blocks care. Emergencies are admitted first; deposits, top-ups and insurer approvals run alongside, with alerts instead of stopped treatment.
Cashless that keeps time. The insurance desk works to the IRDAI 1-hour and 3-hour clocks, prompts enhancement at 90% and warns about room-rent limits before an upgrade.
The family sees the bill. Attendants see the running bill, deposit and discharge progress on the phone, which cuts disputes at the counter.
Beds as a flow. Bed requests, transfers, expected discharges and cleaning tasks sit on one screen, so a vacated bed is ready in minutes.
Charges post themselves. Room, nursing, oxygen, equipment, diet and visits post from the census, charts and orders; pharmacy returns credit before the final bill.
NABH numbers without registers. Occupancy, length of stay, turnover, discharge time, deaths and LAMA come from time stamps, with formulas shown.

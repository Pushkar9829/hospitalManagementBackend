STEP 1Admission advised
From OPD, emergency or planned surgery; reason, stay, bed type
STEP 2Estimate and counselling
Room tariff, package, deposit; estimate e-signed
STEP 3Pre-authorisation
Policy checked; request with documents; insurer replies in 1 h
STEP 4Bed allocated
By category, gender and isolation need; reserved 2 h
STEP 5Admitted
IP number, consents, barcode wristband, attendant pass
STEP 6Pays deposit
UPI, card or cash by bed category; none if cashless approved
STEP 7Received in ward
Nursing assessment in 2 h: allergies, falls and pressure risk
STEP 8Initial assessment
History, plan and orders in 24 h: medicines, tests, diet
STEP 9Orders fulfilled
Medicines issued patient-wise; samples; diet from kitchen
STEP 10Care every shift
Barcode medicine round (MAR), vitals, intake-output, notes
STEP 11Daily rounds
Progress notes, results reviewed, orders changed
STEP 12Charges posted
Room, nursing, services daily; alert at 80% of deposit
STEP 13Enhancement
Raised when bill reaches 90% of the approved amount
STEP 14Discharge planned
Expected date set a day ahead; summary drafted
STEP 15Final bill
Pharmacy returns credited; insurer final approval in 3 h
STEP 16Goes home
Medicines explained, summary and bill on WhatsApp, gate pass
STEP 17Bed turned around
Cleaning task; bed available again in 45 min
STEP 18After discharge
Follow-up booked, feedback, record to MRD, claim filed
IPD end-to-end flow · a planned admission from advice to bed turnaround
Each column is one step in time; each lane is the person or system doing it. Emergency, cashless, government scheme, transfer, LAMA, death and other paths are on the Alternate flows board.

Time stamps captured on every admission
Advised · estimate signed · pre-auth sent and answered · bed reserved · admitted · received in ward · first nursing and doctor assessment · every medicine given · discharge advised · bill final · insurer final approval · patient left · bed cleaned. These give admission time, discharge turnaround and bed turnaround (NABH indicators).
Loop every day of the stay
Steps 9 to 13 repeat daily until discharge is planned. Transfers to another bed or ICU can happen at any point and change the room tariff from that hour.
Blue cards
Steps the system does on its own: daily charges, deposit and approval alerts, follow-up booking, record completion reminders and claim filing.
Insurance timings
IRDAI Master Circular on health insurance (May 2024): insurer decides cashless requests within 1 hour and final discharge approval within 3 hours. The TPA desk screen shows both timers.
